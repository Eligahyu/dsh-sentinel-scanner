# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a professional, welcoming, and technically rigorous community. Be respectful, discuss evidence rather than people, protect confidential security information, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or pressuring someone to disclose an unpatched vulnerability publicly.

## Security-sensitive discussion

Sanitize samples, logs, paths, credentials, and reports before sharing them. Vulnerabilities in dsh-sentinel must be reported through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public issues or discussions.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact methods on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and as promptly as practical.
